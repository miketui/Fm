**Psychological Pricing Notes:**

- $44.99 communicates premium but accessible
- Anchor to $381 bonus value = 85% savings perception

---

**AMAZON KINDLE: $9.99** (Recommended)

**Justification (Why lower on Amazon):**
- Amazon algorithm favors $2.99-$9.99 range for visibility
- 70% royalty tier ($2.99-$9.99) vs. 35% royalty (above $10)
- Competitive pricing (most beauty business books $7.99-$12.99)
- Price-sensitive browsers expect lower ebook prices
- Use Amazon for volume, your site for margin

**Customer Net:** $9.99 × 70% = **$6.99 per sale**

**Volume Assumptions:**
- Year 1: 1,000 sales (Amazon's larger audience)
- Revenue: 1,000 × $6.99 = **$6,993**

**Acceptable Price Differential:**
- Explain on website: "Amazon promotional pricing for wider reach"
- Your site offers: Bonuses, bundles, exclusive pre-order perks
- Customers understand platform pricing differences

**Alternative Kindle Pricing:**
- **$12.99:** If you want higher margin ($9.09 per sale at 70%)
- **$14.99:** Still within competitive range ($10.49 per sale)
- **$7.99:** If prioritizing maximum volume ($5.59 per sale)

**Recommendation:** Stick with $9.99 for optimal balance of visibility + revenue.

---

**OTHER PLATFORMS (Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play, Nook): $14.99-$19.99**

**Response Strategy:**

- Compare to salon education ($200-$500/day workshop)
- Highlight ROI: "If this book helps you raise your prices by just $10 per haircut, it pays for itself in 5 clients"
- Emphasize workbook elements: "128 interactive exercises + 7 journal sections = $50+ value if sold separately"

**Copy Examples:**
- "This isn't just a book—it's a complete professional development system"
- "Less than the cost of one continuing education class, with lifetime access"
- "400+ pages of strategies that could add $10K+ to your annual income"

---

**OBJECTION 2: "I can get similar books cheaper on Amazon."**

**Response Strategy:**
- Differentiate content (faith-based, freelance-focused, workbook hybrid)
- Show comparison table (your book vs. competitors)
- Offer money-back guarantee (30 days, no questions asked)
- Bundle bonuses (website exclusive: $381 value bonuses with pre-order)

**Copy Examples:**
- "Name one other book with 128 quizzes, worksheets, AND faith-integrated business strategy"
- "Most beauty business books are 200 pages of theory. This is 400+ pages of action steps"

**OBJECTION 3: "I'm just starting out, I can't afford this right now."**

- Offer payment plan (future: split into 3 payments of $16.65)

**OBJECTION 4: "I don't have time to read a 400-page book."**

**OBJECTION 5: "Why is it cheaper on Amazon Kindle?"**
