## 🔄 KEY CHANGES FROM ORIGINAL PRD

### Strategic Shift: Paid Membership → Free Community

**BEFORE (Original PRD):**
- Curl Collective: Paid membership ($29/month or $290/year)
- Access to: Discord, live workshops, resource library, mentorship
- Revenue model: Book sales + membership MRR
- Target: 300 paid members ($8,700/month MRR)

**AFTER (Updated V2.0):**
- Curls.Contemp.Collective: **Free membership** (signup + login required)
- Access to: Premium blog posts, download library, monthly newsletter, book samples
- Revenue model: Book sales primary, future upsells (courses, consulting)
- Target: 5,000 free members (larger email list for nurture)

**WHY THE CHANGE:**
- ✅ Lower barrier to entry = 5-10x more signups
- ✅ Larger email list = more book buyers long-term
- ✅ Positions book as primary revenue source ($44.99 vs. $29/month)
- ✅ Future upsells (Phase 2: courses $297-$497, consulting $197-$497/session)
- ✅ Customer LTV: $45 (book) + $297 (course) = **$342 average** (vs. $29/month × 12 × 70% retention = $244)

### Updated User Journeys (4 Flows)

**FLOW A: Blog Visitor → Free Member → Book Buyer** (Primary Growth Engine)
- Entry: Google search "how to price hair services" → Blog post
- Lead magnet: Pricing Calculator (email capture)
- Signup: Curls.Contemp.Collective (free)
- Nurture: 5-email sequence over 21 days
- Conversion: 10% free member → book buyer
- Volume: 10,000 blog visitors → 2,000 members → 200 buyers = **$8,798 revenue**

**FLOW B: Social Follower → Free Member → Book Buyer** (Social-First Traffic)
- Entry: Instagram post → Link in bio
- Signup: Compelling value prop (blog + downloads + newsletter)
- Purchase: Instagram story launch announcement
- Conversion: 25% website visit → signup, 12% member → buyer

**FLOW C: Cart Abandoner → Recovered Customer** (Revenue Recovery)
- Entry: Added to cart, abandoned at checkout
- Recovery: 3-email sequence (1 hour, 24 hours, 72 hours)
- Conversion: 18% recovery rate
- Revenue: $54.99 per recovered customer (38% higher AOV with upsells)

**FLOW D: Book Buyer → Engaged Advocate** (Referral Program)
- Entry: Happy customer, 30 days post-delivery
- Actions: Leave review, generate referral link, share on social
- Rewards: Tiered ($10 credit → $50 credit + consultation → $100 credit + VIP)
- Multiplier: Average 3.5 referrals per advocate = **$202.46 total attributed revenue**
