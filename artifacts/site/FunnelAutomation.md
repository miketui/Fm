## FUNNEL OVERVIEW & STRATEGY

### Conversion Funnel Architecture

```
STAGE 1: Awareness → STAGE 2: Interest → STAGE 3: Decision → STAGE 4: Action → STAGE 5: Advocacy

Visitor (Cold Traffic)
    ↓
Email Subscriber (Lead Magnet / Newsletter Signup)
    ↓
Engaged Subscriber (Opens emails, clicks links, consumes content)
    ↓
Pre-Order Customer (Purchases book with bonuses)
    ↓
Active Community Member (Joins Curl Collective, engages, implements)
    ↓
Book Advocate (Shares testimonials, refers others, creates UGC)
```

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Funnel Stage | Metric | Target |
|--------------|--------|--------|
| Visitor → Subscriber | Email Capture Rate | 3-5% (homepage), 15-25% (blog posts) |
| Subscriber → Customer | Pre-Order Conversion Rate | 8-12% |
| Customer → Community Member | Curl Collective Sign-Up Rate | 35-50% |
| Member → Advocate | Referral Participation Rate | 15-25% |
| Cart Abandonment Recovery | Recovery Rate | 15-20% |

### Email List Segmentation Strategy

**Segments:**
1. **Subscribers (Not Yet Customers)** - Receive nurture sequence, educational content, pre-order promotions
2. **Pre-Order Customers (Not Yet Members)** - Receive post-purchase onboarding, community invitations, bonus delivery
3. **Active Community Members** - Receive exclusive content, event invitations, advanced training
4. **Inactive Subscribers (30+ days no engagement)** - Receive re-engagement campaigns
5. **Cart Abandoners** - Receive recovery sequence
