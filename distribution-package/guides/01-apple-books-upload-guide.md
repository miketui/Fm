## Prerequisites

- [ ] Apple ID with two-factor authentication enabled
- [ ] Apple Books Partner account (free)
- [ ] Completed tax information in iTunes Connect
- [ ] Banking information for royalty payments
- [ ] ISBN (optional but recommended)

---

## Step 1: Set Up Apple Books Partner Account

1. **Visit:** https://www.apple.com/apple-books/apple-books-for-authors/
2. Click **"Get Started"**
3. Sign in with your Apple ID
4. Complete the registration process
5. Accept the Apple Books terms and conditions
6. Provide tax information (IRS W-9 for US, W-8BEN for international)
7. Add banking information for payments

---

## Step 2: Access Books for Authors Dashboard

1. Go to: https://authors.apple.com/
2. Sign in with your Apple Books Partner Apple ID
3. You'll see your dashboard

---

## Step 3: Upload Your Book

### Add New Title

1. Click **"+ Add a Title"** or **"My Books"** > **"Create New Book"**
2. Choose **"Create a new book"**

### Upload EPUB File

1. Click **"Choose File"** under EPUB section
2. Select: `curls-and-contemplation.epub`
3. Wait for upload and validation (1-2 minutes)
4. Apple Books will automatically validate your EPUB
5. ✅ Expect zero errors (your EPUB is validated)

## Step 5: Upload Cover Image

Apple Books extracts cover from EPUB automatically, but you can upload a separate high-res version:

1. Navigate to **"Cover Image"** section
2. Upload: `assets/cover.svg` (converted to high-res JPEG/PNG first)
3. **Requirements:**
   - Minimum: 1400 x 2100 pixels
   - Recommended: 2800 x 4200 pixels
   - Format: JPEG or PNG
   - RGB color space
   - File size: Under 4 MB

**Note:** Your EPUB already includes the cover, so this step is optional.

## Step 6: Set Pricing & Availability

### Pricing
- **Price Tier:** Select your desired price (e.g., $9.99)

## Troubleshooting

### EPUB Validation Errors
✅ Your EPUB passes EPUBCheck with 0 errors, so this shouldn't occur.

If Apple Books reports issues:
- Check the specific error message
- Re-validate with EPUBCheck
- Contact Apple Books support if needed

### Metadata Issues
- Ensure all required fields are complete
- Description should not contain HTML (plain text only)
- Keywords should be single words or short phrases

### Cover Image Issues
- Ensure proper dimensions (minimum 1400 x 2100)
- Use RGB color space (not CMYK)
- Save as JPEG or PNG (not SVG)
