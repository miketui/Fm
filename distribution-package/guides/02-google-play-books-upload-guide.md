## Step 1: Set Up Google Play Books Partner Center

1. **Visit:** https://play.google.com/books/publish/
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign In"**
3. Sign in with your Google account
4. Accept the Google Play Books Partner Agreement
5. Complete your profile information

---

## Step 2: Complete Payment & Tax Information

### Payment Profile
1. Go to **Settings** > **Payment Profile**
2. Select your country
3. Enter business or individual information
4. Add banking information for royalty payments
5. Set payment threshold (minimum $10)

### Tax Information
1. Go to **Settings** > **Tax Information**
2. Complete tax forms (W-9 for US, W-8BEN for international)
3. Verify your tax status

---

## Step 3: Add Your Book

### Create New Book
1. From dashboard, click **"+ Add Book"**
2. Choose **"Upload files"**

### Upload EPUB
1. Click **"Choose Files"** or drag and drop
2. Select: `curls-and-contemplation.epub`
3. Wait for upload (1-3 minutes depending on connection)
4. Google will automatically process and validate your EPUB

## Royalty Information

### Royalty Rates
- **List Price $2.99-$9.99:** 70% royalty
- **List Price above $9.99:** 52% royalty
- **List Price below $2.99:** 52% royalty

**Recommendation:** Price at $9.99 for maximum 70% royalty

### Payment Schedule
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Timing:** 30 days after end of month
- **Minimum Threshold:** $10 USD
- **Payment Method:** Bank transfer (based on your payment profile)

### Example Calculation
- Book Price: $9.99
- Google's Cut (30%): $3.00
- Your Royalty (70%): $6.99 per sale
