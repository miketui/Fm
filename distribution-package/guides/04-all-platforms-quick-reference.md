- **Portal:** https://play.google.com/books/publish/
- **Upload:** EPUB 3.0 directly
- **Metadata:** See `metadata/google-play-books-metadata.txt`
- **Guide:** `guides/02-google-play-books-upload-guide.md`
- **Royalty:** 70% at $9.99, 52% above $9.99
- **Best For:** Android users, global reach

### Amazon Kindle (KDP)
- **Portal:** https://kdp.amazon.com/
- **Upload:** Convert EPUB or upload directly (KDP converts)
- **Metadata:** See `metadata/amazon-kdp-metadata.txt`
- **Guide:** `guides/03-amazon-kdp-quick-guide.md`
- **Royalty:** 70% at $2.99-$9.99, 35% outside range
- **Best For:** Largest audience, Kindle Unlimited

### Kobo
- **Portal:** https://authorize.kobo.com/
- **Upload:** EPUB 3.0 directly
- **Metadata:** See `metadata/kobo-metadata.txt`
- **Royalty:** 70% at $1.99-$12.99, 45% outside
- **Best For:** International audience, accessibility focus

### Barnes & Noble (Nook)
- **Portal:** https://press.barnesandnoble.com/
- **Upload:** EPUB 3.0 directly
- **Royalty:** 65% at $2.99-$9.99, 40% outside
- **Best For:** US market, print-digital bundling

### Draft2Digital (Aggregator)
- **Portal:** https://draft2digital.com/
- **Upload:** EPUB 3.0, DOCX, or plain text
- **Distribution:** Distributes to multiple retailers
- **Royalty:** You keep 85% (they take 15%)
- **Best For:** One-upload-many-platforms approach
- **Reaches:** Apple Books, Kobo, B&N, OverDrive, Scribd, and more

### PublishDrive (Aggregator)
- **Portal:** https://www.publishdrive.com/
- **Upload:** EPUB 3.0 or other formats
- **Distribution:** 400+ stores and libraries
- **Royalty:** You keep 90% (they take 10%)
- **Best For:** Global distribution, analytics

### Smashwords (Aggregator)
- **Portal:** https://www.smashwords.com/
- **Upload:** Formatted Word .doc or EPUB
- **Distribution:** Major retailers + library systems
- **Royalty:** 60% direct sales, varies for distribution
- **Best For:** Libraries, wide retail reach

## Troubleshooting Quick Fixes

### EPUB Validation Issues
✅ **Your EPUB is already validated (0 errors)**

If a platform reports issues:
1. Check specific error message
2. Re-validate with EPUBCheck
3. Contact platform support with error details

### Cover Display Issues
- Ensure cover is embedded in EPUB (✅ already done)
- Verify separate cover meets platform dimensions
- Check RGB color space (not CMYK)

### Metadata Rejected
- Verify no HTML in description (unless platform supports)
- Check keyword/category compliance with platform rules
- Ensure no prohibited content in title/description

### Pricing Issues
- Verify price meets platform minimum/maximum
- Check currency conversions are reasonable
- Ensure tax settings are correct
